Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

In short: Campus Pulse is a student-made app for your school's feed, teacher pages, study groups, chat and calendar. We only collect what the app needs to work. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.

This policy explains what information Campus Pulse ("the app", "we", "us") collects when you use heinzketchup123.github.io/my-first-webpage, how it's used, who can see it, and the choices you have.

1. Information you give us

Your account

  • Email sign-up: your email address, a password and the name you enter. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider (Supabase) and stored scrambled (hashed), never in plain text. We can't see your password.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address and profile picture link with us. We use the name and email to create your account. We don't get your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account (no Gmail, Drive, contacts or calendar).
  • Profile: your display name, your @handle (made from your email) and the school you pick. If a school requires it, a school email address or join code you enter to get in.

Things you post or do in the app

  • Feed posts, comments and emoji reactions.
  • Teacher pages, reviews, ratings, notes and course requirements, and "helpful" votes.
  • Study groups you create or join, and messages in their group chats.
  • Direct messages with friends, friend requests and friendships.
  • Calendar events you add and events you RSVP to.
  • Requests to join a school, and notifications about activity (for example "Maya commented on your post").

2. Information kept only on your device

Some things are saved in your browser's local storage and are never sent to us: your appearance and layout settings, your GPA calculator courses and grades, the focus timer, the last page you were on, whether anonymous mode is on, and how you left the admin panel. Clearing your browser data removes them.

If you turn on Device Alerts, your browser asks your permission to show notifications. They're created by the app on your device while it's open; we don't run a push notification service.

3. How we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, show your school's content, deliver messages and notifications.
  • To keep the app safe: so school admins can remove harmful content and users, and so rules like "only group members can read the group chat" can be enforced.

We don't sell or rent your information, use it for advertising, or build profiles about you.

4. Who can see what

  • People at your school can see the feed, comments, reactions, teacher pages, study groups, events and your display name and @handle.
  • Direct messages can only be read by you and the friend you're talking to.
  • Group chats can only be read by members of that study group.
  • Anonymous mode hides your name from other students on new posts, comments and reviews (they show as "Anonymous Student"). They are still linked to your account in our database, so app admins can see who posted them if needed, for example to deal with abuse.
  • App admins can view and delete content, see a school's list of students, approve or deny join requests, and remove students from a school.

5. Services we use

These companies process data for us to make the app work:

  • Supabase: stores the app's database and handles sign-in.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website files.
  • Google: only if you use Google sign-in.
  • Google Fonts, cdnjs and jsDelivr: deliver the app's font, icons and code library. Like any website, they receive your IP address and browser details when your device downloads those files.

Each follows its own privacy policy. We don't allow them to use your information for their own advertising.

6. How long we keep it

Your account and content stay until you delete them or ask us to delete your account. Notifications are deleted automatically after 60 days. When a study group is deleted, its group chat is deleted with it. Anything you delete is removed from the database. Chat messages can't be deleted from inside the app yet; ask us if you need any removed.

7. Your choices

  • Delete your feed posts and comments, edit or delete your teacher reviews and notes, and edit or delete study groups you host.
  • Turn anonymous mode on or off in Settings.
  • Leave study groups, unfriend people, and change your school.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete your account and everything linked to it (see Contact).

8. Security

The app uses encrypted connections (HTTPS), and database rules that limit each person to the information they're allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't share anything in the app you'd be seriously harmed by if it became public, and use a password you don't use elsewhere.

9. Age

Campus Pulse is meant for students aged 13 and older. If you're under 13, please don't use it. If we learn that someone under 13 has an account, we'll delete it.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For big changes we'll let you know in the app.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests (a copy of your data, corrections, removing messages, or deleting your account), contact an admin at your school in the app, or reach us through the project's page at github.com/heinzketchup123/my-first-webpage. Please don't post personal details there; just ask for a private way to reach us.
