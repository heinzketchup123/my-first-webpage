Terms of Service

Last updated: September 29, 2026

In short: be respectful, keep it honest, don't post other people's private information, and don't try to break the app. Admins can remove content and accounts that break these rules.

These terms apply to your use of Campus Pulse ("the app", "we", "us") at heinzketchup123.github.io/my-first-webpage. By creating an account or using the app, you agree to them. If you don't agree, please don't use the app. Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

1. Who can use it

You must be at least 13 years old. You're responsible for your account, so keep your password to yourself and tell an admin if you think someone else has got into it. Only join a school you actually attend.

2. Be a good member of your school

When you post, comment, review, chat or create groups and events, you agree not to:

  • Harass, bully, threaten or intimidate anyone, including teachers and staff.
  • Post hate speech, or content that's sexual, violent or otherwise inappropriate for a school community.
  • Share anyone's private information (addresses, phone numbers, private photos, grades, etc.).
  • Pretend to be someone else, or post reviews about teachers you haven't had.
  • Post things you know are false, or reviews meant to hurt someone rather than help other students.
  • Share test answers or anything else that breaks your school's academic honesty rules.
  • Spam, advertise, or post links to scams or harmful sites.
  • Use anonymous mode to break these rules. Anonymous posts are still linked to your account, and app admins can see who wrote them.

Your school's own rules still apply to what you do here.

3. Teacher reviews

Reviews should be about the class and the teaching, based on your own experience, and written so they'd be fair to read aloud. Focus on things like workload, clarity and grading, not on someone's personal life or appearance.

4. Your content

You own what you post. By posting it, you let us store it and show it to the people it's meant for (for example your school, your friend, or your study group) so the app can work. You can delete your feed posts, comments and teacher posts at any time. Don't post anything you don't have the right to share.

5. Don't misuse the app

Don't try to get into other people's accounts or data, get around the app's rules or limits, overload it, copy large amounts of its content, or use bots to use it.

6. Admins and moderation

There are three levels of admin: school admins look after one school, district admins look after every school in their district, and app admins look after the whole app. Admins can remove content and remove students from the schools they look after, and app admins can suspend or delete accounts that break these terms or put others at risk, with or without warning. If you see something that breaks the rules, tell an admin.

7. Google sign-in

If you sign in with Google, you also have to follow Google's terms. We only receive your name, email address and profile picture link from Google.

8. The app is provided "as is"

Campus Pulse is a student project. We work to keep it running and accurate, but we can't promise it will always be available, error-free, or that information posted by other users (like reviews, events or GPA estimates) is correct. The GPA calculator gives an estimate only; your school's official records are what count. We may change, pause or stop parts of the app at any time.

9. Limitation of liability

To the extent the law allows, we aren't responsible for content posted by other users, or for any loss or harm from using or not being able to use the app.

10. Ending your use

You can stop using the app at any time and ask us to delete your account (see the Privacy Policy). We may close accounts that break these terms.

11. Changes

We may update these terms. We'll change the date at the top, and for big changes we'll let you know in the app. If you keep using the app after a change, you accept the new terms.

12. Contact

Questions about these terms? Contact an admin at your school in the app, or reach us through the project's page at github.com/heinzketchup123/my-first-webpage.
